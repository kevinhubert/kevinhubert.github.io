About

I’m a frontend architect with 15+ years leading React platforms, design systems and complex async workflows — the foundations that let a team ship interfaces that feel fast and work for everyone.

Right now I’m at Everlywell, leading the frontend of a greenfield telehealth app and building the AI agents and Claude Code skills my team uses every day. Before that, I led frontend architecture at Findhelp.org and spent nearly nine years at BuildASign.

I studied philosophy at Texas A&M, and it shows up in how I work: breaking fuzzy problems into clear structure, writing precise context for AI agents, and judging their output critically. Outside work, I’m a husband and dad of two, and I’m usually building something or learning something new.
